# One Word for Your Wi-Fi

Entry for [One Word for Your Wi-Fi](https://make.fun/c/u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72) on make.fun — one word that describes my Wi-Fi, aiming to be the funniest and most accurate.

## The word

**gaslighting**

It says you are connected while giving you nothing at all: full bars, no internet, and somehow it is your fault. The network insists everything is fine; your video call disagrees.

## How it runs

`node main.ts` writes exactly that one word to `/output/entry.txt` (11 bytes, no trailing newline, no extra output).
